# Research integrity policy

1. Frozen results are never overwritten or silently rerun.
2. Development and held-out phases use separate seeds and are labelled clearly.
3. Failed criteria remain in the permanent record.
4. New protocols receive new names, seeds, hashes and claims.
5. Model generation, memory retrieval, contract transport and systems stability are reported separately.
6. Interface or evaluator defects are disclosed and do not retroactively change a frozen result.
7. Public summaries must link to receipts, hashes or reproducibility assets.
8. Negative results may guide a new experiment, but the new experiment does not repair the old one.
